Contact Us

You can contact us by email at contact@todolist.github.io. We aim to reply to every message within a few working days.

When you write, please include the name of the tool or guide you are asking about, and a short description of the question or problem. That helps us answer quickly and accurately.

Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information by email. We will never ask for those details.

For general feedback, suggestions for new tools, or corrections to a guide, the same email address works. Thank you for helping us improve the site.
